What is Interval Level Accuracy (ILA)?

Interval Level Accuracy (ILA) measures how precisely your forecast matches actual call/contact volume at every time interval (e.g., every 15 or 30 minutes). Unlike daily accuracy, ILA captures intraday staffing gaps that daily metrics miss entirely.

Formula: ILA = 100% − |Actual Volume − Forecast Volume| ÷ Actual Volume × 100%

Achieving >90% ILA at interval level is considered best-in-class for call center workforce management. BasitWFM AI is designed to help teams reach and maintain this standard.

How to Improve Forecast Accuracy in WFM?

Forecast accuracy improves when you combine these techniques:

  • Recency-weighted historical data — give more weight to recent weeks (W-1, W-2) to capture current trends.
  • Interval-level distribution splits — instead of splitting daily forecasts evenly, use actual intraday patterns.
  • Rolling window analysis — identify peak load windows and pre-position resources before they hit.
  • Systematic error tracking — compare your model vs baseline continuously and adjust weights accordingly.
  • Intraday recasting — refresh forecasts midday using actuals to adjust remaining intervals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an intraday planning tool?
An intraday planning tool helps WFM planners manage real-time staffing during the day by comparing actual vs forecasted volume and recommending agent movements or schedule adjustments.

How does BasitWFM AI differ from standard WFM tools?
BasitWFM AI adds an AI insight layer on top of standard ILA calculations — automatically generating risk alerts, staffing actions, and email communications that normally require manual analysis.

Do I need a backend or server?
No. BasitWFM AI runs entirely in your browser. Upload or paste your data and all calculations happen instantly on your device.

Can I customise the interval size and weights?
Yes — you can set any interval size (15 min, 30 min, custom), start/end time, and adjust the 5-week recency weights in the Settings panel.

Understanding Rolling Windows

The 4-hour rolling window engine aggregates weighted distribution % across overlapping 4-hour blocks, advancing every 60 minutes. This reveals which parts of your operating window carry the heaviest load — critical for pre-positioning agents before peaks hit. Windows with the highest % contribution require maximum headcount and minimum schedule adherence breaks.
